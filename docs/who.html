World Health Organization

WHO

Part of the global network of authorities working for the common good

Remit

The WHO's stated purpose is to achieve the highest possible level of health for all the world's people, defining health as "a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity."

Individuals can make financial contributions to support WHO through the independent WHO Foundation.
