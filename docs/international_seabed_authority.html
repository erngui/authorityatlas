International Seabed Authority

ISA

Part of the global network of authorities working for the common good

Remit

ISA has the mandate to ensure the effective protection of the marine environment from harmful effects that may arise from deep-seabed-related activities.

The Area protected by ISA/UNCLOS covers around 54 per cent of the total area of the world’s oceans.
